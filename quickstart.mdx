## Overview

This guide sets up Kao with the hosted Kao Bot, the recommended path for most servers. You do not need a Discord Developer Portal application or a bot token. Setup takes about five minutes.

## Prerequisites

- A Discord account
- A Discord server where you have the **Manage Server** permission

<Info>
On the Free plan, the hosted Kao Bot can join up to 2 servers. See [Billing and plans](/guides/billing#plan-limits) for other limits.
</Info>

## Step 1: Sign in with Discord

Go to [usekao.com](https://usekao.com) and sign in with Discord.

Kao asks Discord for your account identity, email address, server list, and your roles in those servers. Kao uses this to show the servers you can manage and to check who can use Kao in each server. Kao does not read your messages.

## Step 2: Pick your first emotes

Setup opens with **Pick your first emotes**, a list of trending 7TV emotes.

1. Select a few emotes you want in your server.
2. Click **Continue**.

Your picks stay selected while you invite Kao, even if you leave for Discord and come back.

## Step 3: Choose how Kao joins your server

Choose **Use Kao Bot**. It is marked **Recommended**.

Choose **Bring Your Own Bot** only if you want your own bot identity. See [Choose a bot setup](/concepts/bot-system) before you pick it.

## Step 4: Invite Kao

1. Under **Where should Kao join?**, select your server.
2. Click **Invite Kao**. Discord opens in a new tab or in the Discord app.
3. Review the permissions in Discord and approve the invite.
4. Return to Kao. Kao checks the server automatically and shows **Kao joined this server.**
5. Click **Continue to emotes**.

If the Discord window closed before you approved, click **Reopen Discord**. If you already invited Kao, click **I've already invited Kao**.

<Warning>
If Discord does not list your server, your account does not have **Manage Server** there. Ask a server admin to give you that permission or to invite Kao.
</Warning>

## Step 5: Import your emotes

1. Under **Review your emotes**, check your picks and the target server.
2. Click **Add N emotes**, where N is the number of emotes you picked.
3. Watch the preparation and upload progress until the import finishes.

To finish setup without importing, choose **Skip importing for now**.

Discord limits each server to about 50 new emojis an hour. If you picked more, Kao queues the rest and adds them as slots free up.

## Optional: join the Kao Support Discord

The last step can offer **Join the Kao Support Discord**. Click **Continue to Discord** to join, or **Go to dashboard** to skip. Setup is already complete either way.

<Tip>
You can leave setup at any time with **Skip setup**. To start again later, open **Settings** and use **Restart setup** under **Advanced Options**.
</Tip>
