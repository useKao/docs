## Overview

This page highlights selected product changes that affect Kao customers. For the complete release history, visit the [Kao changelog](https://usekao.com/changelog). For smaller updates and support notices, join the [Kao Discord server](https://usekao.com/api/discord/invite).

## November 2026

### A new mobile dashboard

On phones and tablets, **Overview**, **Servers**, **Emotes**, and **Import** now sit in a bottom tab bar. **More** opens every other page. Search, sheets, and forms fit smaller screens, and the dashboard uses neutral gray surfaces.

### Search 7TV by tag

Type `#` and a tag, such as `#cat`, in the **Import** search bar to find 7TV emotes by tag. Combine tags with a name, such as `dance #cat #hat`. See [Search 7TV by tag](/guides/importing-emotes#search-7tv-by-tag).

### Import defaults

Choose a starting appearance and framing in **Settings** > **Import defaults**. New imports, **Quick add**, collection imports, the Emote Creator, and setup start with it. See [Import emotes](/guides/importing-emotes#set-your-import-defaults).

### Sharper animated emotes and accurate queue times

Large animated emotes that Kao used to refuse now import, and more keep every frame at full size. Animated emotes stay animated when you restore a backup. When work waits for Discord's hourly emoji limit, Kao shows a finish time that updates every minute.

### Clearer automations

Rotations, backups, and syncs each have their own page with settings and history. Rotation runs show which emotes were added or removed, and you can retry a failed emote with **Try again**. Syncs no longer delete an emote when its replacement will not fit in the server.

### Safer deletes and confirmations

Every destructive action now asks before it acts, including deleting a creation, removing emotes from a collection, clearing activity, unlinking a browser, and canceling an operation. If something fails, the dialog stays open with the error and a retry. The deletion progress window has its own **Cancel** button.

### Privacy Policy update

The Privacy Policy now has a section on using Kao in a Discord server. From December 6, 2026, a server's managers can see who added or removed emotes with Kao. See [Account and privacy](/guides/account-management#privacy-basics).

## October 2026

### Kao in Discord

Type `/kao` in any channel to add, manage, protect, and back up emotes without opening a browser. Every import shows a review card first, with 10 minutes to undo. See [Use Kao in Discord](/guides/bot-commands).

### Emote polls

Let members vote on new emotes, from a single **Yes** or **No** to a ballot of up to 8 emotes. Kao adds the winners for you. See [Emote polls](/guides/emote-polls).

### Streamer emotes

Follow a streamer and Kao keeps a collection matched to their 7TV emote set. Kao can post a daily digest, add new emotes, or keep your server in sync. See [Streamer emotes](/guides/streamer-emotes).

### Protected emotes

Protect emojis so Kao never removes them during rotations, syncs, restores, or bulk deletes. If someone deletes a protected emoji in Discord, Kao adds it back. See [Protect emojis](/guides/managing-emojis#protect-emojis).

### A rebuilt Emote Creator

Choose **Guided** for four short steps or **Editor** for every layer on one screen. Drafts save as you go, and animated creations keep every frame. See [Emote Creator](/guides/emote-creator).

### Servers, Team, and Kao roles

Each server has its own page with **Overview**, **Commands**, **Polls**, **Team**, and **Activity**. Share a hosted Kao Bot server with teammates through a Discord role, and choose who can import, manage emotes, or change settings with Kao roles. See [Server settings](/guides/server-settings).

### Notifications and the browser extension

A new notification centre lets you choose whether each kind of notice arrives in Kao, by email, or as a Discord DM. The Kao browser extension for Chrome adds 7TV emotes to your server in one click. See [Notifications](/guides/notifications) and [Browser extension](/guides/browser-extension).

### Fresh swap rotations and safer restores

The **Fresh swap** rotation adds the newest emotes and removes the oldest unprotected ones only when the server is full. Restores now show a preview of what will be added and removed before you confirm.

### BetterTTV and EmojiGG search removed

BetterTTV and EmojiGG closed their search to other sites, so Kao can no longer search them. Emotes you already saved from them still import. Animated FrankerFaceZ emotes now import with their animation.

## September 2026

### Easier setup

Setup pairs each step with a Discord preview, marks servers that already have Kao installed, and shows separate preparation and upload progress for your first import.

### Account deletion and privacy requests

Delete your account from **Settings** with a 14-day cancellation period, and download a deletion receipt when it completes. Submit privacy requests and follow their status from your dashboard. See [Account and privacy](/guides/account-management).

### Redesigned billing

The **Billing** page brings your plan, usage, payment details, and plan comparison together.

### Support inside Kao

Open **Support** from the dashboard to check service status, contact the team, or report a problem with an optional screenshot.

## August 2026

### Clearer bot health warnings

The dashboard now explains when a bot is missing permissions, offline, still starting, or no longer in a server. Warnings link to the relevant bot or server action so you can resolve the problem without guessing.

### Support when you need account-specific help

The dashboard now includes a Support area with setup context, service-status guidance, FAQs, troubleshooting paths, and direct support channels. The public [Help Center](https://usekao.com/help) and documentation remain available for general questions.

### Public service status

Kao now provides a shared public status page and clearer status links in the dashboard so you can check for an ongoing service issue before retrying a failed action.

### Better import previews and progress

Import workflows now include appearance profiles, Discord-scale previews, clearer queued and optimizing states, and background processing for difficult animated emotes. You can minimize an import and follow it from Activity while work continues.

### Account preferences

Your selected dashboard language and theme can now be saved to your Kao account.

## July 2026

### Safer retries

Supported dashboard actions now handle repeated network retries more safely, reducing the chance of duplicating a change when a request result is delayed.

### Current 7TV compatibility

Kao updated its 7TV integration to remain compatible with current source identifiers and API behavior.

## June 2026

### Collection, rotation, and import fixes

Collection search results can now be added without invalid URL errors, rotation setup refreshes target servers after bot linking, and bulk imports only count emotes that fit in the server before showing upgrade prompts.

### Dashboard interface polish

Dashboard settings, navigation, notifications, search, and emoji tools now look more consistent, with better translations across supported languages.

### Crypto checkout discounts

Eligible coupons now work with crypto checkout as well as card checkout. Kao shows the discounted crypto price before sending you to NowPayments, and some trial-extension coupons can activate access without a crypto invoice.

### Fresher coupon banners

When Kao launches or updates a coupon, the pricing page and promotional banners show the current offer straight away.

## May 2026

### More accurate import rate-limit handling

Hosted Kao Bot imports now follow Discord's emoji limits more accurately, so large imports are less likely to wait longer than necessary. Queued imports also keep clearer Discord retry timing in the import modal, operations dock, and Activity.

### Queued animated import reliability

Animated imports that need background preparation now stay attached to their original import batch when Discord rate limits are active. Import progress and Activity now show the same queue timing, including a clearer ready state when a queued item is due.

### More reliable animated imports

Animated emotes that need extra preparation now move to background processing sooner, so imports keep showing progress while they are prepared.

### Clearer queue status

Bulk operation progress is easier to recover after navigating around the dashboard. The dashboard and Activity now agree more consistently about whether work is waiting on your daily allowance, server capacity, or a Discord rate limit.

### Faster, safer imports

Imports, rotations, syncs, and restores now share clearer Discord capacity handling. When work has to wait, Kao can show whether the delay is related to your quota, server capacity, or Discord's emoji limits.

### Clearer bot setup

Bot setup now shows the right setup choices for your account as soon as the page opens.

## April 2026

### Shared server access

Teams can now share server access so trusted members can help manage emojis without owning the bot or billing account.

### More reliable imports

Imports now do a better job of preparing images, avoiding duplicate work, showing progress, and recovering from temporary failures.

### Multi-language support

Kao is available in more languages, with a translation contribution flow for community improvements.

### Account deletion and export

Account settings now support full data export and account deletion flows so you can keep a copy of your data or remove your account.

### Emoji safety and stability

Kao improved animated emote handling, permission checks, and dashboard accessibility across core workflows.

## February 2026

### Emote Creator

You can create custom emotes by layering overlays on a base emote, preview the result, save it to your gallery, and import it into a server.

### Multiple bot management

You can manage more than one BYOB bot from the same account and assign bots to different servers.

### Activity log

The Activity page now tracks imports, backups, rotations, syncs, and deletes so you can review progress and results.

### Plan management

Kao added more plan controls, trial support, coupon support, and clearer plan limits in the dashboard.

### Import improvements

Search and import workflows improved with broader provider filtering, clearer server names, and more accurate backup snapshots.

### Initial release

Kao launched with Discord sign-in, Bring Your Own Bot setup, emote search, imports, server emoji management, bulk deletion, and custom bot setup.

Supported sources at launch included 7TV, BetterTTV, and FrankerFaceZ.
