## General

### What is Kao?

Kao is a Discord emoji manager. It imports emotes from 7TV and FrankerFaceZ, keeps servers up to date with streamers' 7TV emotes, and lets you manage, protect, back up, rotate, and sync emojis. You can use it from the web dashboard, from Discord with `/kao`, or from the browser extension.

### Is Kao free?

Yes. Kao has a Free plan and a paid Pro plan. Pro raises limits and adds rotations, sync groups, team access, real-time streamer alerts, and poll schedules. See [Billing and plans](/guides/billing#plan-limits).

### Which emote sources are supported?

You can search and import from 7TV and FrankerFaceZ, including animated emotes. Kao can no longer search BetterTTV or EmojiGG, but emotes you already saved from them still import. In Discord, you can also import emojis and images from a message.

### Do I need to create my own Discord bot?

No. Most servers use the hosted Kao Bot. Create your own bot only if you want your own bot identity or BYOB-only features such as staff notifications. See [Choose a bot setup](/concepts/bot-system).

## Setup

### What Discord permissions does Kao need?

The bot needs **Create Expressions** to add emojis and **Manage Expressions** to rename or delete them. It also needs permission to read and send messages so it can reply to commands. Kao never asks for Administrator. See [Discord permissions](/concepts/bot-system#discord-permissions).

### Why can I not invite the hosted bot to my server?

You need the **Manage Server** permission in that server. Ask a server admin to invite Kao or give you the permission.

### Why does Kao ask me to update Kao Bot's permissions?

Servers that added Kao Bot before the late October 2026 update need to approve its new permissions once. Click **Update in Discord** on the server's **Overview**.

### Can I use the same BYOB bot in several servers?

Yes. Invite the bot to each server. On Free, one bot can work in up to 3 servers.

## Kao in Discord

### How do I use Kao in Discord?

Type `/kao` in any channel of a server where Kao is installed. See [Use Kao in Discord](/guides/bot-commands).

### Who can use Kao in my server?

Members with a Kao role: **Importer**, **Emote manager**, or **Kao administrator**. The server owner and Administrators always have access. Set roles on the server's **Commands** page. See [Kao roles](/guides/server-settings#kao-roles).

### Can I undo an import?

Yes. Imports started in Discord can be undone for 10 minutes from the review card or from **Activity**. Browser extension **Quick add** imports can be undone for a short time from the extension's toast.

## Imports and emojis

### How many emojis can I import?

Three limits apply:

- Your plan's daily limit: 100 emotes on Free, 500 on Pro.
- Discord's limit of about 50 new emojis per server per hour.
- The server's free slots, which depend on its boost level.

| Server boost level | Static emojis | Animated emojis |
|--------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| No boost | 50 | 50 |
| Level 1 | 100 | 100 |
| Level 2 | 150 | 150 |
| Level 3 | 250 | 250 |

### Can I import animated emotes?

Yes, when the server has free animated slots. Kao fits animated emotes under Discord's 256 KB limit and keeps every frame.

### What do appearance and framing do?

**Appearance** controls how large the subject looks: **Auto** or **Larger**. **Framing** controls the edges: **Fit** shows the whole image and **Trim** removes empty space. You can set defaults in **Settings** > **Import defaults**.

### How do I stop Kao from removing an emoji?

Protect it. Click **Protect emoji** on the emoji's card. Rotations, syncs, restores, and bulk deletes skip protected emojis. Free includes 10 protected emojis per server.

### Why did an import fail?

Common causes:

- The server has no free slots of that type
- The bot is missing **Create Expressions**
- The emoji name is invalid or already used
- The source file is unavailable

**Activity** shows the reason for each failed item.

## Automation

### What are collections?

Reusable groups of emotes. Use them for imports, rotations, sync groups, and poll schedules.

### What are streamer emotes?

Follow a streamer and Kao keeps a collection matched to their 7TV emote set. Kao can alert your server about new emotes, add them, or keep the server in sync. See [Streamer emotes](/guides/streamer-emotes).

### What are backups?

Point-in-time copies of a server's emojis. Use them before large changes, or to copy emojis to another server.

### How do rotations work?

Rotations change a server's emojis from your collections on a schedule, or when a followed streamer adds emotes. Rotations need a paid plan.

### What are sync groups?

Sync groups copy a collection or source server's emojis to target servers when you click **Sync now**. Kao matches emojis by name, so a target emoji with the same name and type is kept as it is. Sync groups need a paid plan.

## Account and billing

### How do I sign in?

Sign in with Discord at [usekao.com](https://usekao.com).

### Which payment methods are accepted?

Cards through Stripe, and cryptocurrency through NowPayments.

### Can I use coupons with crypto?

Yes, if the coupon covers crypto payments. Kao shows the discounted price before you pay.

### Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Open **Billing** and use **Cancel subscription**. You keep your plan until the end of the billing period. Kao pauses work beyond Free limits but does not delete it.

### Where do I change the dashboard language or theme?

Open **Settings** and go to **Preferences**.

### Can teammates help manage a server?

Yes, on a paid plan. Open the server's **Team** section and choose a Discord role. Members with that role can manage the server from their own Kao account.

### Can I export or delete my account?

Yes. Open **Settings**. Use **Request data export** under **Privacy and data**, or **Delete account** in the **Danger** zone. Deletion has a 14-day cancellation period. See [Account and privacy](/guides/account-management).

### I don't have a Kao account. Can I ask you to delete my data?

Yes. Email [privacy@usekao.com](mailto:privacy@usekao.com) or open a support ticket in the [Kao Discord server](https://usekao.com/api/discord/invite).
