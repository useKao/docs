## Start with the docs

Use the [FAQ](/resources/faq) for short answers and [Troubleshooting](/resources/troubleshooting) when something is not working. The [Quickstart](/quickstart) is the fastest way to set up the hosted Kao Bot.

## Check service status

Before retrying something that fails across several servers or pages, check the [Kao status page](https://status.usekao.com). It shows whether Kao is operational, degraded, under maintenance, or down.

The dashboard also shows a notice when Discord or 7TV is having problems that affect Kao.

## Get help from the dashboard

If you are signed in, open **Support** from the dashboard. It shows:

- Kao's current status and any alerts
- **Needs your attention**: problems with your servers or account that you can fix
- **Contact the team**: links to ask in Discord, email support, or report a bug
- **Common questions** and links to the Help Center and these docs

## Contact Kao

| Channel | Use it for |
|---------|------------|
| [Kao Discord server](https://usekao.com/api/discord/invite) | Community help, product discussion, or a private support ticket |
| [support@usekao.com](mailto:support@usekao.com) | Account-specific questions or private details. The team replies within two days |
| [Report a bug](https://usekao.com/report) | A reproducible bug or broken flow. Signed-in users can also use **Write a report** on the **Support** page |
| [Send feedback](https://usekao.com/feedback) | Ideas and general feedback. Requires sign-in |
| [privacy@usekao.com](mailto:privacy@usekao.com) | Privacy and data requests, including if you do not have a Kao account |
| [Help Center](https://usekao.com/help) | General answers that apply to any Kao account |

To check a privacy request you already made, go to [usekao.com/privacy-requests](https://usekao.com/privacy-requests) and enter the token from your receipt.

## What to include

When you contact support, include:

- What you were trying to do
- The exact error message, including any support reference code
- Which server or workflow was affected
- What you already tried
- A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem

<Warning>
Never send your Discord password or a BYOB bot token to anyone, including Kao support.
</Warning>
