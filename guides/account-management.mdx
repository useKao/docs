## Overview

Account settings help you control your Kao setup, dashboard preferences, and privacy choices.

## Open settings

Open **Settings** from the dashboard sidebar. Today, this area is also where you can restart setup if you want to choose a different bot path.

## Dashboard preferences

Open **Settings** and choose **Preferences** to save your dashboard choices to your account.

- **Theme** changes the dashboard accent color.
- **Language** changes the dashboard language.

These preferences follow your account when you sign in again. They do not change your Discord server's settings.

## Export your data

Use data export when you want a copy of the information Kao stores for your account.

If an export control is visible in your dashboard, use it there. If it is not visible, contact support and request an account export.

Exports can include profile details, bot records, collections, rotations, sync groups, activity history, billing references, and other account data.

<Info>
Large accounts can take longer to export.
</Info>

## Delete your account

Account deletion permanently removes your Kao account and associated data.

1. Export your data first if you want a copy.
2. Use the dashboard deletion control if it is available.
3. If the control is not visible, contact support and request account deletion.

<Warning>
Account deletion is permanent. Your bots may stop working with Kao, server configurations are removed, and the action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Deletion certificate

When available, Kao provides a deletion certificate that records the deletion request and processing status. Keep it if you need proof that the request was made or completed.

## Policy updates

When Kao updates legal policies, the dashboard may ask you to review and accept the new version before continuing.

## Cookie preferences

Use cookie settings from the site footer or account area to review available cookie choices and save your preference. Optional analytics are enabled only when you choose them. Read the [Cookie Policy](https://usekao.com/cookies) and [Privacy Policy](https://usekao.com/privacy) for details.

Kao supports multiple interface languages. The language switcher can also suggest a language based on your browser or region until you choose a preference.

## Privacy basics

Kao uses Discord sign-in and stores the account and server management data needed to provide the service.

Kao does not:

- Read your Discord messages
- Sell your data
- Show bot tokens after they are saved
