## Overview

The **Settings** page holds your personal Kao settings. It is one scrolling page with these sections:

| Section | What it controls |
|---------|------------------|
| **Preferences** | Dashboard theme and language |
| **Import defaults** | The starting appearance and framing for new imports. See [Import emotes](/guides/importing-emotes#set-your-import-defaults) |
| **Browser extension** | Linked browsers and **Quick add**. See [Browser extension](/guides/browser-extension) |
| **Notifications** | Where each kind of notice reaches you. See [Notifications](/guides/notifications) |
| **Privacy and data** | Data export and privacy requests |
| **Advanced Options** | **Restart setup** |
| **Danger** zone | **Delete account** |

## Preferences

- **Theme** changes the dashboard's accent color.
- **Language** changes the dashboard language. Kao supports 12 languages.

These preferences are saved to your account and follow you to other devices. They do not change your Discord server's settings.

## Export your data

1. Open **Settings** and go to **Privacy and data**.
2. Under **Your data export**, click **Request data export**.

Kao downloads a JSON file with the information it stores for your account, such as your profile, bots, collections, automation, activity, and billing references.

## Make a privacy request

Use a privacy request when you want Kao to act on your personal data, for example to get a copy or remove it.

1. Open **Settings** and find **Privacy requests**.
2. Click **Make a request**, choose what you need, and click **Submit request**.
3. Follow the request's status in **Settings**. Open requests also appear on your dashboard.

When a download is ready, click **Verify with Discord to download**. Each download link works once. You can **Withdraw** a request while it is open.

If you do not have a Kao account, email [privacy@usekao.com](mailto:privacy@usekao.com). To check a request you already made, go to [usekao.com/privacy-requests](https://usekao.com/privacy-requests) and enter the token from your receipt.

## Delete your account

<Warning>
Deleting your account locks it immediately. You have 14 days to cancel. After that, deletion begins and cannot be undone.

Some changes happen as soon as you schedule deletion and are not undone if you cancel:

- Sessions, Discord sign-in credentials, BYOB bot tokens, webhook secrets, and notification preferences are removed.
- Linked browsers are unlinked, and your **Quick add** history is removed.
- On every server that uses one of your BYOB bots, community announcements and the **Rotations**, **Backups**, and **Collections** server features are turned off.
- Every rotation is turned off and every sync group is paused. Paused sync groups cannot be resumed.
</Warning>

1. Export your data first if you want a copy.
2. Open **Settings** and click **Delete account** in the **Danger** zone.
3. Optionally, tell Kao why you are leaving.
4. Review what will happen and click **Continue**.
5. Click **Verify with Discord**.
6. Type the confirmation text, check the box, and click **Schedule account deletion**.

### What happens when you delete your account

- Your account is locked. You cannot sign in or start new work during the 14 days.
- BYOB bots stop working with Kao immediately.
- Emojis already in your Discord servers stay there. They belong to the server, not your Kao account.
- Subscriptions stop renewing. Deletion does not issue a refund automatically.
- Kao keeps only the minimum records it needs for legal, financial, or security reasons.

### Cancel or check a deletion

Kao shows a deletion status page after you schedule deletion. Use it to check progress or click **Cancel deletion** during the 14 days. Canceling requires you to verify with Discord again.

Canceling unlocks your account, but the changes made when you scheduled deletion stay in place. After canceling:

1. Sign in with Discord again.
2. Paste your BYOB bot tokens again with **Update token**.
3. Set your notification preferences again and link your browser extension again.
4. On servers that use your BYOB bots, reconnect community announcements and turn features back on under **Commands** > **Server features**. On servers you do not own, ask a Kao administrator there to do this.
5. Turn your rotations back on.
6. Paused sync groups cannot be resumed. Create them again, or contact support.

When deletion finishes, click **Download deletion receipt** on the status page to keep proof that it was completed.

For full details, see [Deleting your Kao account](https://usekao.com/help/account-deletion) in the Help Center.

## Policy updates

When Kao updates its Terms or Privacy Policy, the dashboard may ask you to review and accept the new version before you continue.

## Cookie preferences

Open cookie settings from the site footer to review your choices. Choosing **Essential Only** turns off optional analytics. Automatic error diagnostics still run so Kao can fix problems. Read the [Cookie Policy](https://usekao.com/cookies) for details.

## Privacy basics

- Kao uses Discord sign-in. It stores the account and server data it needs to provide the service.
- When you use Kao in a server, Discord sends Kao your Discord ID, name, avatar, roles, and permissions in that server. Kao uses them to check what you may do and records what you did.
- Message commands such as **Import emotes** only read the message you choose.
- From December 6, 2026, a server's managers can see who added or removed emotes with Kao in that server.
- Kao does not sell your data and never shows a bot token after you save it.

Read the [Privacy Policy](https://usekao.com/privacy) for full details.
