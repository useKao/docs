## Overview

Kao has a Free plan and a paid plan, Kao Pro. For higher limits, Kao also offers an Enterprise plan. Contact [support@usekao.com](mailto:support@usekao.com) for details. Your plan sets limits such as how many emotes you can add per day, how many servers can use the hosted Kao Bot, and whether you can use rotations and sync groups.

Use the **Billing** page in the dashboard to see your plan and usage, compare plans, pay, and manage your subscription. For current prices, see the [Pricing page](https://usekao.com/pricing).

## Plan limits

| Limit | Free | Pro |
|-------|------|-----|
| New emotes per day | 100 | 500 |
| Emotes per import | 25 | 100 |
| Servers with the hosted Kao Bot | 2 | Unlimited |
| Your own bots (BYOB) | 1 bot, in up to 3 servers | 10 bots, in up to 50 servers |
| Collections | 2, up to 50 emotes each | 25, no emote limit |
| Backups | 2 | 15 |
| Rotations | Not included | 15, as often as every 2 hours |
| Sync groups | Not included | Included |
| Team access | Not included | Included |
| Protected emojis per server | 10 | Unlimited |
| Open emote polls | 2, up to 4 emotes each | 10, up to 8 emotes each, with schedules |
| Followed streamers | 1, with a daily digest | Unlimited, with real-time alerts |
| Emote Creator creations | 3 | 200 |
| Emote Creator uploads | 10 | 100 |

Discord's own limits apply on every plan. Each server can take about 50 new emojis an hour. See [Discord limits](/concepts/overview#discord-limits).

<Info>
Limits can change. The [Pricing page](https://usekao.com/pricing) and your **Billing** page always show the current limits for your account.
</Info>

## The Billing page

| Section | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Plan** | Your current plan and renewal date, with **Change plan** and **Update payment method** |
| **Usage** | What you have used against your plan's limits |
| **What Kao Pro adds** | The features you would get by upgrading |
| **Compare plans** | Opens the plan comparison and checkout |
| **Cancel subscription** | Cancels a paid plan |

If the Kao team gave you a plan, the **Plan** section says so and shows when it ends.

## Upgrade

1. Open **Billing** and click **Compare plans** or **Change plan**.
2. Under **Billing interval**, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**. Yearly costs less per month.
3. Optionally, enter a code under **Coupon** and click **Apply**.
4. Choose a payment method and continue to checkout.

### Pay by card

Choose **Credit / Debit Card** and click **Continue to Stripe**. Card subscriptions renew automatically. Most card payments activate your plan immediately.

### Pay with crypto

Choose **Cryptocurrency** and click **Pay with Crypto**. Kao opens a NowPayments invoice where you choose the coin.

- Each crypto payment covers one billing term. It does not renew automatically.
- You have 7 days to pay an invoice.
- Kao activates your plan after NowPayments confirms the full payment. Network confirmation can take a while.
- If you renew the same plan early, you keep the time you have left.
- Partial payments, payments in the wrong coin, expired invoices, and refunds do not activate a plan automatically. Contact support with your order details.

<Warning>
If you pay with crypto while a card subscription is active, Kao shows **Payment received; plan change needs review**. Your current plan stays in place until support reviews the payment.
</Warning>

## Coupons and trials

Coupons can apply to card payments, crypto payments, or both. Kao shows the discounted price before you pay. Some coupons extend or activate access without a crypto invoice.

Kao sometimes offers trials or plan gifts. When one applies to your account, the **Billing** page and your notifications show it.

## Change or cancel your plan

To change plans, click **Change plan**. To switch to a cheaper option at your next renewal, choose **Downgrade at renewal**.

To cancel:

1. Open **Billing** and go to **Cancel subscription**.
2. Click **Continue to cancel**.
3. Confirm on Stripe's page.

You keep your plan until the end of the current billing period. Then your account moves to Free.

## What happens when a paid plan ends

Kao pauses work that goes beyond Free limits. It does not delete it.

- Rotations over the limit show **Paused by plan**.
- Extra collections become read-only.
- Hosted Kao Bot servers beyond the Free limit are paused.
- Backups stay restorable, and emoji protection stays in place.

Upgrading again brings everything back.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Card payment failed | The card was declined | Check the card details, or use **Update payment method** |
| Crypto payment is pending | The network has not confirmed the payment yet | Wait, then refresh **Billing** |
| Crypto invoice expired | The 7-day payment window passed | Start a new checkout from Kao |
| Wrong coin or amount was paid | The payment did not match the invoice | Contact support with your order details |
| Plan did not activate | The payment is still processing | Refresh **Billing**. If it stays inactive, contact support |
| Coupon did not apply | The coupon does not cover this plan, interval, or payment method | Check the coupon terms and try again |
