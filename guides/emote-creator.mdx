## Overview

The Emote Creator combines a base emote with overlays to make a new emote. Your work saves as a draft while you go, and finished emotes appear on your **Your creations** page. From there you can add them to a server or download them.

Open **Create** in the sidebar to start.

## Choose a mode

| Mode | Best for |
|------|----------|
| **Guided** | Your first emote. Four short steps |
| **Editor** | Full control. Every layer on one screen, with handles, layer order, keyboard shortcuts, and undo |

Select **Remember my choice** to skip this screen next time. You can switch modes later.

## Make an emote in Guided mode

1. **Pick a base**: choose a 7TV emote, one of your creations, or an image you upload.
2. **Add overlays**: try overlays such as hats, signs, and reactions on top of the base.
3. **Fine-tune**: adjust where each overlay sits, its size, tilt, and see-through level. Add a style such as an outline, glow, or shadow.
4. **Name and save**: give the emote a name and save it.

## Use the Editor

The Editor shows the canvas, the layer list, and a live Discord preview together. Select a layer to move, resize, or rotate it with its handles. Drag layers in the list to change which one is in front. Click **Save emote** when you are done.

The library has four tabs:

| Tab | Contents |
|-----|----------|
| **Emotes** | 7TV emotes and your own creations to use as a base |
| **Overlays** | Decorations grouped into categories such as Popular, Party, Weather, Hands, Signs, and Reactions |
| **Uploads** | Your own images. PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP, up to 4 MB each |
| **Templates** | Quick layouts for the selected overlay, such as Corner badge, Tilted, or Top hat |

You can use up to 5 layers in one emote.

## Drafts

Kao saves your work as a draft while you edit. Drafts follow you to every device where you sign in. Open a draft to **Continue**, or **Discard** it. You can keep up to 25 drafts.

If a save fails, click **Try again** to save the same work without making another edit. Your local copy stays on the current device in the meantime.

If you edit the same draft on two devices, Kao asks whether to **Keep mine** or **Use the other**.

## Animated emotes

Animated creations keep every frame. Kao fits them under Discord's 256 KB limit when it can. A creation over the limit can still be saved and downloaded, but not added to a server.

## Use your creations

Open **Your creations** to see everything you have made. Filter by **All**, **Animated**, **Static**, or **Not on a server**.

For each creation you can:

- **Edit** or **Remix** it. Both save a new creation and keep the original.
- **Download** it.
- Add it to a server. Each server shows **Add**, **Added**, or **No room**.
- Delete it.

Creations follow the same naming rules and slot limits as imported emotes. Kao uses your [import defaults](/guides/importing-emotes#set-your-import-defaults) when it adds a creation to a server.

## Plan limits

| Limit | Free | Pro |
|-------|------|-----|
| Saved creations | 3 | 200 |
| Uploaded images | 10 | 100 |

## Design tips

Discord shows emojis at small sizes.

- Keep compositions simple and high-contrast.
- Remove tiny details that disappear when shrunk.
- Check the live Discord preview before you save.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Emote looks unclear in Discord | Too much detail at small sizes | Simplify layers and check the Discord preview |
| Overlay is hidden | It is behind another layer | Move it higher in the layer list |
| Save failed | A temporary connection problem | Click **Try again**. If Kao cannot confirm the save, check your drafts first |
| Creation shows **No room** | The server has no free slots of that type | Free a slot on the **Emotes** page |
| Creation cannot be added | It is larger than 256 KB | Simplify the animation or download it instead |
