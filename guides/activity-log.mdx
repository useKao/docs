## Overview

The **Activity** page shows what Kao is doing now, what it has done, and how much of your plan's allowance you have used. Use it to follow imports, deletes, restores, rotations, syncs, streamer follow updates, and poll results.

## Happening now

Work that is running or waiting appears at the top of the page.

- Click **Cancel** to stop an operation. Kao asks you to confirm.
- For queued emotes, use **Add first** to move an emote ahead of the others, or **Remove** to take it out of the queue.

While work runs, the operations dock at the bottom of the dashboard shows progress on every page. You can minimize an import and reopen it from the dock or from **Activity**. Use **Clear completed** in the dock to hide finished work.

## History

The **History** list shows past work. Filter it by:

- **All** or **Needs a look**
- **Where it started**: **Dashboard**, **Discord**, **Browser extension**, or **Automation**
- Server

Open an entry to see the server, where it started, when it started, and how long it took. Entries for rotation runs and restores link to their full details.

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Waiting for you** | Kao needs you to confirm or fix something |
| **Waiting** | Kao accepted the work and is waiting to run it, for example for Discord's hourly limit |
| **Running** | Kao is processing items. Shows "N of M done" while it runs |
| **Completed** | Everything finished. Shows counts such as "N added" or "N deleted" |
| **Partly done** | Some items succeeded and some failed |
| **Failed** | The operation could not finish |
| **Cancelled** | Someone stopped the operation |

## Undo a Discord import

Imports started in Discord can be undone for 10 minutes. Open the import in **Activity** and click **Undo import**. You can also undo it from the review card in Discord.

Imports from the browser extension's **Quick add** can be undone for a short time from the extension's toast.

## Usage and limits

The **Usage and limits** panel shows:

- **Today's limit**: how many emotes you can still add today on your plan
- **This hour, per server**: how many new emojis Discord will accept this hour, and when the next slot frees up
- **Last 7 days**: your recent usage

## Why work can wait

Emoji work may wait when:

- The server reached Discord's limit of about 50 new emojis an hour. Kao shows "Waiting for Discord's hourly emoji limit" and an estimated finish time.
- Your plan's daily import allowance is used up. Kao shows "Waiting for your daily import limit to reset".
- Another restore, rotation, or sync is already running on the same server.
- An animated emote is still being prepared in the background.

## Clear activity

On **Activity**, press and hold **Clear** to hide the entries in your list. On **Overview**, the **Recent Activity** card has **Clear all**, which asks you to confirm, and each row's menu has **Remove**.

Clearing activity only hides entries. It does not stop running work or undo any changes.

## Share activity with support

If support asks about an activity entry, share its status, the server name, when it started, and any message it shows. Never share bot tokens, payment details, or private Discord messages.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Work stays **Waiting** | Discord's hourly limit, your daily allowance, or other work on the server | Check the reason and estimated time shown |
| Entry shows **Partly done** | Some items failed | Open it to see which items failed and why |
| Entry shows **Failed** | Missing permissions, a full server, or a source problem | Read the message, fix the cause, and try again |
| **Undo import** is missing | The 10-minute undo window has passed, or the import did not start in Discord | Delete the emojis on the **Emotes** page instead |
