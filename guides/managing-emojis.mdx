## Overview

Use the **Emotes** page to keep a server's emoji list clean. From there you can:

- View static and animated emojis and free slots
- Search and filter the emoji list
- Rename emojis and limit them to roles
- Protect emojis so Kao never removes them
- Delete one emoji or many

You can do most of this from Discord too, with `/emotes`. See [Use Kao in Discord](/guides/bot-commands#manage-emotes-from-discord).

## Before you begin

- The bot needs **Manage Expressions** to rename or delete emojis.
- Create a [backup](/guides/backups) before large cleanups.

## Open a server's emojis

1. Open **Emotes** in the sidebar.
2. Select the server.
3. Use search or filters to find an emoji.

Each emoji card shows the image, name, and buttons for **Edit emoji**, **Protect emoji**, and **Delete emoji**.

## Rename or restrict an emoji

1. Click **Edit emoji** on the card.
2. Change the **Emoji Name**.
3. Optionally, choose **Role Restrictions** to limit the emoji to certain Discord roles.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

Emoji names must be 2 to 32 characters long. They can contain letters, numbers, and underscores, and cannot start with a number.

<Info>
Renaming an emoji does not change older Discord messages that already used it.
</Info>

## Protect emojis

Protected emojis are emojis Kao must never remove. Rotations, syncs, restores, and bulk deletes all skip them.

To protect an emoji, click **Protect emoji** on its card. To protect several, select them and click **Protect** in the selection bar. To remove protection, click **Turn protection off**.

If someone deletes a protected emoji in Discord, Kao adds it back. Kao stops after 3 restores in a day and tells you. Click **Restore again** to turn automatic restore back on.

| Plan | Protected emojis per server |
|------|-----------------------------|
| Free | 10 |
| Pro | Unlimited |

Moving to a smaller plan never removes protection you already set.

## Delete emojis

<Warning>
Deleting an emoji is permanent. Restoring it from a backup creates a new emoji with a new Discord ID, so older messages that used it still show it as missing. Create a backup first if you may need it later.
</Warning>

To delete one emoji:

1. Click **Delete emoji** on the card.
2. Confirm the deletion.

To delete several emojis:

1. Select emojis with the checkbox on each card, or use **Select all** or **Select visible**.
2. Click **Delete** in the selection bar.
3. Review the list on the **Preview** step.
4. Confirm the deletion.

A progress window shows each deletion. Click **Cancel** to stop the remaining deletions.

Protected emojis cannot be deleted until you turn protection off. Emojis managed by a Discord integration, such as Twitch subscriber emojis, are skipped.

## Plan your slots

Discord counts static and animated emojis separately, so Kao shows both.

If a server is almost full:

- Remove duplicates and unused emojis.
- Keep animated slots for emotes that need movement.
- Save reusable emotes into a [collection](/guides/collections) before you delete them.
- Protect the emojis your community relies on.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Server is not listed | Kao is not in the server, or it was not shared with you | Add the server, or ask the owner for team access |
| Rename or delete fails | The bot lacks **Manage Expressions** | Use **Update in Discord** on the server's page, or fix the bot's role |
| "Protected. Turn protection off to delete it." | The emoji is protected | Click **Turn protection off**, then delete it |
| Deleted emoji still shows in Discord | Discord is caching the old image | Restart Discord |
| You deleted the wrong emoji | Deletion is permanent | Restore it from a backup, if you have one |
