## Overview

Kao tells you when work finishes, when something needs fixing, and when there is news about your account. You choose where each kind of notice reaches you.

This page covers your personal notifications. To post updates in a Discord channel for your server, see [Server settings](/guides/server-settings#notifications-byob).

## Read your notifications

Click the bell in the dashboard header to open the notification centre. Filter by **All**, **Unread**, **Announcements**, or **Alerts**, and use **Mark all read** to clear the unread count.

Click **View all notifications** to open the full Notifications page. There you can:

- Switch between **Inbox** and **Archived**
- **Search notifications**
- Filter by **Categories** or **Unread only**
- Select notices and **Archive** them, or **Move to inbox**

## Choose how notices reach you

1. Open **Settings** and go to **Notifications**.
2. For each category, choose **In Kao**, **Email**, **Discord DM**, or a combination.
3. Kao saves each change as you make it.

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Announcements | Product news and release notes |
| Account & billing | Plan changes |
| Servers & bots | Missing bot permissions, **Server Members Intent** turned off, a bot removed from a server, or a protected emote no longer restored |
| Automation | Import finished, rotation failed or paused by your plan, or sync failed |
| Streamers | Streamer follow paused |
| Security | A new browser was linked to your account |

Click **Show each notice** to choose settings for individual notice types within a category. Some important notices are marked **Always on** and cannot be turned off.

## Delivery details

- **Email** goes to the email address on your Discord account. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link for its category.
- **Discord DM** comes from Kao Bot. You need to share a server with Kao Bot to receive DMs.
- **In Kao** shows the notice in the notification centre.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No emails arrive | Email is off for the category, or your Discord email is out of date | Check **Settings** > **Notifications** and your Discord email |
| No Discord DMs arrive | You do not share a server with Kao Bot, or your DMs are closed | Join a server with Kao Bot and allow DMs from server members |
