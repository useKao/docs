## Overview

Emote polls let your members decide which emotes get added to the server. Kao posts the poll in Discord, counts the votes, and adds the winning emotes automatically.

There are two kinds of poll:

| Poll type | How it works |
|-----------|--------------|
| Single emote | Members vote **Yes** or **No** on one emote |
| Ballot | Members pick their favorites from 2 to 8 emotes on a private ballot. The top picks win |

## Before you begin

- Kao must be in the server, with permission to post in the poll channel.
- To change poll settings on the dashboard, you need to manage the server in Kao: own its bot, or be an admin of its hosted Kao Bot install.
- To start and manage polls in Discord, you need the Kao administrator role and the **Manage Server** permission, or a **Poll managers** role.
- The server needs free emoji slots for the winners.

<Info>
If you do not see **Polls** on a server's page, polls are not available for that server yet.
</Info>

## Set up polls

1. Open **Servers**, select the server, and choose **Polls**.
2. Open the **Settings** tab.
3. Make sure **Polls are on** is turned on.
4. Choose a **Poll channel**.
5. Set **Who can vote**: **Anyone**, **Only these roles**, or **Everyone except these roles**.
6. Choose your **Poll managers**. They can start, end, cancel, and retry polls in Discord.
7. Save your settings.

You can also set **Poll length**, **Votes needed**, **Yes votes needed to pass (%)**, **Progress updates**, and a **Log channel**.

## Start a poll

You can start a poll in three ways:

- **Dashboard:** open the server's **Polls** section and click **New poll**.
- **`/kao`:** open **More** > **Polls** > **New poll**.
- **Message command:** right-click a message that contains 7TV or FrankerFaceZ links, choose **Apps**, then **Start an emote poll**.

Kao posts the poll in the poll channel you chose.

## When a poll ends

Kao adds the winning emotes automatically. If an emote cannot be added, Kao explains why and offers **Try adding again**.

If a poll ends with too few votes, you can still choose **Add it anyway**. Use **End now** to close a poll early, or **Cancel poll** to stop it without adding anything.

Find every poll's result on the **Past polls** tab.

## Poll schedules

On the Pro plan, Kao can start polls for you on a repeating schedule. Open the **Schedules** tab and choose a source: one of your collections or 7TV trending emotes.

## Plan limits

| Limit | Free | Pro |
|-------|------|-----|
| Open polls at once | 2 | 10 |
| Emotes per poll | Up to 4 | Up to 8 |
| Poll schedules | Not included | Included |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **Polls** section is missing | Polls are not available for the server yet | Check again later |
| Poll does not post | Kao cannot post in the poll channel | Check the channel permissions for Kao's bot |
| Winner was not added | The server is full or the bot lacks **Create Expressions** | Free a slot or fix permissions, then use **Try adding again** |
| Members cannot vote | **Who can vote** excludes their roles | Review **Who can vote** in poll settings |
| You cannot start a poll | You reached your plan's open poll limit | End a poll or upgrade |
